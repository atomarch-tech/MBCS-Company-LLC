Welcome to MBCS

Hi, I'm Tunde,

Owner and Company Manager of MBCS Company LLC

MBCS Company, LLC is a family-owned, certified building contracting company established on April 19, 2017, in West Palm Beach, Florida. Since our founding, we've served homeowners, businesses, and organizations throughout Palm Beach County, helping clients achieve their renovation and construction goals.

Our mission is to consistently meet customers' needs while fostering strong, long-lasting relationships — many of which evolve into lasting friendships. Word-of-mouth referrals have driven our growth from day one.
